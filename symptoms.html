Symptoms

Understanding the Signs

Symptoms associated with PCOS can vary in type and severity.

Remember: One or more symptoms does not by itself confirm a diagnosis.
01

Irregular Periods

PCOS may be associated with irregular menstrual periods.

02

Menstrual Changes

Hormonal activity may be associated with changes in menstrual bleeding.

03

Hormonal Changes

Hormonal changes may affect menstrual and reproductive health.

04

Skin Changes

Hormonal changes associated with PCOS may affect the skin.

05

Hair Growth Changes

Hormonal activity may affect hair-growth patterns.

06

Fertility Concerns

PCOS can be associated with difficulty becoming pregnant.
